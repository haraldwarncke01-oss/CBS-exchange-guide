Sources & interpretation: The website reads its comparison data from local CSV snapshots stored in this GitHub repository. CBS colors and labels come from the original Excel workbook. Current GPA, language, calendar, housing and related exchange requirements are a local snapshot of CBS MoveON Regular / Undergraduate agreements checked on 22 September 2026; Fall 2027 place counts are shown where CBS has already published them. Eleven historical workbook entries are no longer present in that current MoveON list and are shown without current requirements rather than guessed. Climate is a local snapshot of NASA POWER 1991–2020 2 m air-temperature climatology. ARWU 2026 has been checked against the complete official published list of 1,000 institutions: matched CBS partners show the official exact rank or rank band, while confirmed absences show “Not in top 1000”. Living cost is a local snapshot of Numbeo's 2026 Mid-Year country-level Cost of Living + Rent Index, shown relative to Denmark. ShanghaiRanking · NASA POWER · Numbeo
